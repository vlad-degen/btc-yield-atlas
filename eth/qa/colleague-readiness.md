## Findings worth presenting

- Reservoir's October 2025 book was about $15.91M, its outer Aave debt about $10.35M, and its srUSD-collateralized inner loan about 90.26M USDC before pending accrual. These are distinct financing layers rather than additive investor capital.
- Yearn WETH has a Spark ETH-debt loop with about 7.98 times collateral leverage, a 1.0633 health factor and 14.18% of parent NAV. The strategy remains in parent accounting while absent from the default withdrawal queue.
- YieldBasis has about 27.815M crvUSD of actual AMM debt. Its 64.136M crvUSD allocated funding amount is a different quantity. Staked and unstaked claims divide one pool.
- The autoETH 30% demand simulation fails a specific withdrawal pricing constraint. The measured error does not prove an asset deficit.
- An hgETH share packages an rsETH loan-pool claim. Aggregate loans, physical rsETH and reserved adapter assets reconcile its $13.52M book; 173 historical registry entries are not 173 active loans.
- The USDC lifecycle's earlier $1M cash deposit must be separated from its two $350,000 borrowed lots. Assigning the entire vault income to the loans would overstate carry. Performance fees already reflected in paid redemption are not deducted twice.

An unmapped book amount is not classified as a loss or deficit. Nominal or oracle agreement does not establish executable exit proceeds.
