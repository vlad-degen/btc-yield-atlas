ETH yield: briefing for the team

Snapshot: 2 October 2026, 23:59:59 UTC. Monthly history: October 2024 through September 2026. Presentation reviewed 5 October; underlying document captures retain their own dates.

Start with the market

The default market selection measures 21.158 million ETH equivalents / $57.214 billion across 59 observed protocol rows. The catalogue has eight strategy groups. Lending and CDP are optional financing layers; including them brings observed exposure to 28.177 million ETH equivalents / $76.195 billion across 80 observed rows.

The reader ledger excludes JustLend’s Tron mapped-ETH representation from every category, historical sum and chain chart. The underlying Ethereum backing and redemption route were not verified. Its original observations remain in the source ledger and separate dossier.

These balances contain linked claims on reused ETH. The fixed-block custody graph now measures 457,182 ETH in distinct native-ETH holdings and canonical WETH across examined Ethereum venues, with another 25,176 ETH in the finalized withdrawal queue. Reserve cash can be idle; this restricted custody floor cannot stand in for unique capital earning yield across the whole market. Aave and Spark wstETH lender claims alone repeat 2.350 million ETH equivalents of Lido backing.

Four major liquidity adapters still lack broad historical token breakdowns. A separate extension follows the same 28 Uniswap V3 and Curve pools through 700 successful pool/month observations. Basis has no measured ETH row at T. Ethena's dated disclosures establish all-asset backing and a changing portfolio, without an exact-T ETH basis allocation.

In the default selection, exposure declines 5.13% in ETH equivalents between October 2024 and September 2026. Staking and restaking together decline 4.30%, while the two examined carry parents grow 53.88%. New book claims and coverage affect growth. The constant-cohort view holds protocol membership fixed; neither view is a ledger of outside deposits.

Follow the dollar leg

Liquid's measured weETH/RLUSD loan costs 4.21% APR, or 4.30% in the annual-equivalent convention used in the calculator. The separately dated destination feed reports 3.00% base APY and 2.59% reward APY. Removing rewards makes the dollar spread negative.

Under the stated full-parking scenario, including a 2.27% staking baseline and 0.35% annual NAV fee, annual ETH income is 2.75% with reported rewards and 1.09% without them. The no-reward carry contribution before the manager charge is -0.84 percentage points. These are fixed-rate scenarios for one measured loan leg.

The historical ledger adds a separate answer over 7 August to 2 October, 56.29 days. The RLUSD destination claim grows 145,919 RLUSD, captured RLUSD loans accrue 405,517 RLUSD of cost, and tracked accounts receive 172,802 RLUSD in reward cash. In PYUSD the corresponding components are 164,823, 226,325 and 150,679 PYUSD. Claim and loan principal differ. Reward receipt dates do not identify their earning periods. These measured components cannot simply be combined into whole-product carry profit.

Transaction receipts trace part of the financing through same-token and currency-changing deposits. Historical share ownership also measures gross own-credit overlap: some loan interest can return to the consolidated borrower through its lending destination. That overlap is shown separately, before destination fees and rate caps, rather than treated as independent income.

The venue matters. Aave V3 USDC costs about 13.93% APR on Ethereum at T, versus 4.65% on Base, 3.94% on Arbitrum and 3.83% on Optimism. Actual model curves and reserve cash constrain capacity. A cheaper quoted chain also introduces its own execution and transfer requirements.

Broader market evidence

The expanded funding atlas measures 60 dollar reserves across 15 Aave/Spark instances on 12 chains, with 320 dated annual-rate observations. Aave Ethereum USDT costs 4.38% APR, compared with USDC at 13.93%. Their physical reserve cash is about $183.29 million and $6.33 million. Currency, lending venue and available liquidity all affect the same financing decision.

The credit registry adds 131 Compound, Euler and Fluid market rows. Its 31 simple Fluid routes with only ETH-family collateral have 76.703 million nominal dollar-token units of debt. Dollar reserve totals and whole Euler/Compound vault debt cannot be treated as ETH-only carry capital. The later holder screen resolves selected fixed-T position views for 350 Aave/Spark accounts with enabled ETH-family collateral and at least $1 million of dollar debt; the use of those loans requires separate tracing.

TAU had about 3.04 million USDC of historical nested debt in January, but only 0.022132 USDC across the examined positions at T. Reservoir's October 2025 inner loan owed about 90.26 million USDC, while its outer Aave account owed about $10.35 million and its product book was $15.91 million. Those separate financing layers explain leverage; they do not add investor capital.

The product atlas covers ten additional families and 25 selected cases, including fixed maturity, automated lending, LP, options and custody products. Recovery and closed products remain dated historical cases. The report distinguishes a documented mechanism, a funded position and a measured income result. The coverage assessment gives the complete boundary.

What the deeper contract work changes

The two largest unique addresses in the measured Aave/Spark cohort owe $212.36 million and $205.88 million. Seven sampled cash loans lead to owners, Maker debt repayment or USDC/USDT conversion. An ETH-collateral dollar loan is not automatically a yield investment. The borrower-use investigation shows the transactions and exact attribution limits.

Three financed lifecycles close the destination-income and funding-cost calculation. The two borrowed USDC lots contribute 91.840383 USDC of allocated vault income against 239.889887 USDC of accrued loan cost at redemption, a -148.049504 USDC funded-leg result before gas. The separate PYUSD case returns 1.948867 PYUSD of investment income but incurs 2.347298 PYUSD of financing cost including remaining debt, giving -0.398431 PYUSD before gas. Neither is whole-wallet profit; the USDC loans are valued at destination redemption rather than observed repayment. Performance fees already reflected in paid redemption are not deducted twice.

Across a common 732-day window, stETH's book grows 5.505%, osETH 5.096%, mETH 5.251%, Yearn WETH 4.389% and autoETH 4.182%. These are accounting-share comparisons excluding external rewards and execution. They do not establish complete investor underperformance. The additional financial histories disclose each denominator and exit test.

Yearn's book includes a Spark ETH-debt loop with 7.98× collateral leverage, 1.0633 health factor and about 14.18% of parent NAV. It is absent from the default withdrawal queue. mETH's lending buffer holds about 8.49% of controlled book value; its available-balance view differs from physical buffer ETH. YieldBasis has about 27.815 million crvUSD of actual AMM debt, distinct from 64.136 million crvUSD of allocated funding. Those distinctions matter for both the source of return and exit capacity.

The hgETH case adds a $13.52 million rsETH loan-pool book. Loans, physical rsETH and reserved adapter assets reconcile the parent accounting. Four historical endpoints also show that a nominal ETH share can lose book value. Individual borrower payers, repayments and dollar-carry use remain separate research questions.

Carry development before the product chapters

The main chart now shows all seven measured product books across 24 completed months. Liquid supplies the early large book; Rocksolid and Reservoir become material in September 2025; Concrete’s large claim appears in December. TAU later unwinds its dollar debt, while Liquity grows and Rocksolid enters Closing. A second chart lens reproduces the two adapter parents in Market. These are whole-book histories and overlapping claims, not a reconstructed historical carry allocation or new-deposit series.

Compare the five largest measured carry-design products

Concrete Delta, Liquid ETH, Rocksolid, Liquity ETH Carry and Royco ETH lead the examined census by whole-product book NAV. The first two account for about 96.81% of measured book NAV across the seven products with published carry evidence. This concentration measure is not verified carry equity.

Each chapter uses the same order: key metrics, contract-linked flow, actors and delays, income payers, capital and return history, share-holder distribution, borrowing legs and event timeline. Contract fees and later published policies are dated separately. Concentrated custody does not establish beneficial ownership or new investor deposits.

Liquid ETH's 730-day ETH book return is 6.85%, versus 5.49% for stETH. The measured excess is 1.36 percentage points for the whole book; the historical contribution from each strategy is not isolated. The main Aave loop has 13.3 times collateral leverage relative to account equity and a narrow rate buffer. The dollar destinations also introduce crypto-collateral and private-credit dependencies.

Present the study in order

Use the eight chapters: Answer → Market → Carry math → Top 5 → Other carry → Risks → Playbook → Data. The main page carries the research narrative and evidence exhibits; detailed articles and downloadable data support each chapter.

The practical question is whether a product delivers enough extra income after fees, incentives and exit costs to justify its financing, credit and control dependencies. The Playbook translates the measured constraints into income scenarios, payer diligence, operating rules and partner criteria.

Before discussing investor access, open the exit-demand exhibit. It compares 1%, 10% and 30% of each whole book with historical token payouts. Concrete's tested requests enter a queue. Rocksolid has been in Closing since 29 September and rejects new requests. Liquity's tested 1% withdrawal call succeeds; 10% and 30% calls revert. Royco's immediate route is unavailable at T. Liquid's aggregate unwind is untested, while its captured Ethereum queue has 2,822 receipt-verified payouts. Rocksolid has 428 and Liquity 93. Historical payments establish actual cash delivery through the examined routes; they do not promise today's demand will clear.
