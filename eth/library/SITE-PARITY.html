ETH-specific accounting

ETH already pays staking income, so the carry calculator includes an explicitly dated staking baseline. Borrowing ETH to buy more staking exposure is different from borrowing dollars against ETH. Spot/short basis hedges the underlying ETH price and has a dollar return denominator.

The market measure is overlapping ETH-family exposure. Lending and CDP are off by default as optional financing layers. Basis remains unmeasured in the frozen adapter ledger; its later Ethena disclosure is separate. Product book NAVs are not added on top of their protocol parents. Historical portfolio weights, private payouts and unique external capital are not inferred from totalAssets.

A missing observation stays missing. Contract deployment, a funded balance, a zero adapter token subtotal and a product shutdown are different events. No closure is asserted without evidence.
