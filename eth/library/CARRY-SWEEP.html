Data: ZZPH0ZZ, one row per position where ETH-family collateral backs dollar debt (at least 100 ETH). Scripts: tools/eth/carry_sweep/. Raw reads: raw/eth/carry-sweep-2026-10-08/.

Result

Open products that borrow dollars against ETH and are not counted as carry hold 12,134 ETH against $14.0M of dollar debt. Without the NEMO USDC operator (a dollar vault) and the closed TESS vault, the figure is 9,281 ETH. Method

  1. Lending split accounts. Every account in lending_split_accounts.csv with A2+B2 of at least 100 ETH was read at the snapshot block (classify.py):
  2. code type (EOA, EIP-7702, contract), EIP-1167 and EIP-1967 implementations
  3. about 30 view functions: Safe owners and threshold, DSProxy cache, ERC-4626 asset, BoringVault hook, Mellow vault
  4. Blockscout and Etherscan names
  5. Safe modules (classify2.py)
  6. contract owners of DSProxies and DPMs
  7. the latest ERC-20 counterparties of every Ethereum EOA and Safe (counterparties.py)
  8. Lagoon safe() and Upshift subaccounts, operators and strategists from the two public registries (registries.py)
  9. Other venues.
  10. Curve, all Liquity v1/v2 forks in the DefiLlama registry, Sky/Maker CDPs: 100% of debt read.
  11. Euler v2 on 15 chains: 100% of debt read. Silo v1/v2, Gearbox v3 and Dolomite: no position of 100 ETH except the Dolomite Safe.
  12. Aave v3 on Optimism, Avalanche, Gnosis, Linea, Plasma, Sonic, Scroll, Mantle, Celo, zkSync and Metis.
  13. Compound and Fluid on L2s, and the 100 to 187 ETH band of the split venues.
  14. Owners classified the same way (classify_venues.py). Assembly: build.py.
  15. Counted means listed in data/eth/netmap/carry_lending_snapshot.json, plus Liquity ETH Carry and Rocksolid.
