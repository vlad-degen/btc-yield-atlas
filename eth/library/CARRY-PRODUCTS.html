The final edition adds capital, earned income and investor exit evidence. Its fixed-block custody graph, common-window cash-flow ledgers and receipt-verified payout history extend the original scope of this chapter. Use that exhibit for the completed measured answer and its evidence boundaries.

This establishes a large manager-issued accounting book, not a broad investor base. Share contract, reconciled holder ledger.
