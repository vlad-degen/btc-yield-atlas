Top-5 ETH: holders, keys, fees, terms

Snapshot T = 2 Oct 2026 23:59:59 UTC, Ethereum block 26,108,081. Pulled 7 Oct 2026. Same template as the BTC Kraken chapter: depositors, keys and delays, fee history, terms and loss order, dated events.

Data: ZZPH0ZZ (holders_monthly.csv, holder_buckets.csv, keys.csv, fees.csv, events.csv; Liquid has holder files only). Scripts and raw pulls: raw/eth/gap-2026-10-07/keys-holders/ (scripts/, logs/, rpc_log.jsonl, http/docs/).

Address note: the YieldBasis WETH LT is 0x2b9c9f3b…3cea. 0x2b2a612c… in the brief is a transaction hash, not the LT.

Findings

  1. Avant is controlled by one EOA. 0xd4d23209…57cb has no contract code at T. It is:
  2. owner of avETH, so it can add any minter (unlimited avETH);
  3. admin of savETH (cooldown, blacklist, moving a restricted holder's balance);
  4. admin of the minting contract;
  5. owner of both CCIP bridge pools.

Nothing it does has a delay. Avant's docs say its keys are MPC wallets; that cannot be checked on-chain.

  1. The Avant admin shortened the savETH exit 30 times without notice. It cut the 24h cooldown to 60 seconds on 30 occasions between 20 Apr and 27 Sep 2026, 219 hours in total. The longest window was 30 Jul to 6 Aug (166 h).
  2. Reserve fund at T: 38.01 savETH (about 40 ETH), 0.36% of savETH.
  3. Junior avETHx: 1,353.65 MAX avETH units, holding no avETH or savETH on Ethereum.
  4. One Avant address holds 37.8% of the product. EOA 0x3d461625…7904 holds savETH directly, through a Gearbox credit account and as Morpho savETH/WETH collateral (a leveraged loop).
  5. Owners: 109 after look-through, but the top 10 hold 92.6%.
  6. Bridged out: 16.3% of savETH sits in the CCIP pool for holders on other chains.
  7. Lido Earn ETH has no timelock on any key.
  8. Lazy Vault Admin (Safe 5-of-8): gives itself a role and uses it in the same transaction. It did this on 3 Mar, 18 Apr (paused all deposit queues), 7 Jul and 23 Jul.
  9. Proxy Admin (Safe 5-of-8): can upgrade all five core contracts instantly. It upgraded the share token on 2 Mar 2026.
  10. Pause path: a 0-second timelock holds 16 pre-scheduled pause calls. One executor is the Mellow Pauser, a 1-of-8 Safe.
  11. Curator Safe: 3-of-6 on-chain; Lido's docs say 3/5.
  12. Lido Earn changed fees three times in two months, with no delay.
  13. The fee path ran 0 → 10% + 1%/yr (1 Jul) → 0 (21 Jul) → 15% + 0.2%/yr (3 Sep, current).
  14. Fee shares minted to the Treasury Safe: 154.28 earnETH in total, of which 114.73 came in the 20 days of July.
  15. YieldBasis code is immutable; its parameters move by veYB vote.
  16. Vote rules: 7-day vote, 55% support, 30% quorum. EarlyExecution mode lets a vote execute before the 7 days end, and there is no separate timelock.
  17. Emergency Safe (5-of-9): can kill the market. emergency_withdraw stays open.
  18. Limit setters: two contracts, HybridVaultFactory and LTMigrator, can raise the crvUSD allocation without a vote.
  19. The YieldBasis admin fee is about 40%, not 10%, but none has been taken since 20 Jul.
  20. Why 40%: the fee follows the staking ratio, 1 - 0.9 x sqrt(1 - staked share). At T 56.35% of LT is staked, giving 40.54%; the monthly range was 30% to 43%.
  21. Why nothing is taken: all 5 fee withdrawals since 20 Jul minted 0 LT, so the staked side is still below its high-water mark. Only 5.47 LT was ever taken, all in June.
  22. YieldBasis looks spread out until you look through its holders.
  23. Direct: the gauge holds 56% and the top 10 hold 93.3%.
  24. Look-through (gauge stakers and HybridVault owners resolved): 407 owners; top 1 holds 21.8% (owner of HybridVault 0xbaec9eb1…), top 10 hold 70.7%.
  25. Liquity ETH Carry is run by one Safe 2-of-3 with zero delays.
  26. What the Safe holds: owner, guardian, atomist and fuse-manager (it decides which protocols the strategist may touch).
  27. No delays: the execution delay is 0 on every role, there is no ADMIN_ROLE holder, and the vault implementation is immutable (EIP-1167 clone).
  28. Fees, 28 May: performance 2% → 10% and management 0.3% → 0.5%.
  29. Fees, 19 May: 0.2% each on deposit, instant withdrawal and withdrawal request.
  30. Concentration: one Safe 3-of-6 holds 36.9%. Rocksolid's execution account holds 12.1%, and Rocksolid entered Closing on 29 Sep.
  31. Liquid ETH holder counts are mostly dust. Ethereum has 7,793 holder addresses (Optimism, which adds 1,511, is not counted here).
  32. Dust: 82.4% of addresses hold under 1 ETH and together own 0.26% of capital.
  33. Large holders: 11 addresses hold 70.3%; two unlabelled EOAs hold 47.3%.
  34. Legacy contract: the old Liquid1 migration contract still holds 3.19% (4,431 ETH).
  35. Over time: the address count doubled in Oct 2025 (4,635 → 9,710) with dust. Holders with at least 0.01 ETH fell from 4,439 to 3,151.

Method and limits

  • Logs. Transfer and admin-event logs come from Tenderly eth_getLogs over full ranges. Calls are archive eth_call at T or at month-end blocks (the same blocks as economic-dollar-loans.csv, plus computed blocks for June to September 2024). Role names come from contract constants and IPOR Roles.sol. Two Mellow role hashes (0x877766a8…, 0xfc199f68…) are unresolved.
  • Holder counts include dust and contracts. holders_ge_0.01eth in the monthly CSV filters the dust. The Liquid figures are Ethereum only.
  • ETH prices are book marks. The Lido monthly ETH uses the oracle report at each block. Avant avETH is valued at face (1 ETH).
  • EOA ≠ single key. An EOA can be an MPC wallet; the on-chain data cannot tell. Avant says it uses MPC.
  • Documents were read on 7 Oct 2026. Pages are saved under raw/eth/gap-2026-10-07/keys-holders/http/docs/. Policy text and contract state are kept apart. Where they differ, the contract value at T is used: the Lido curator threshold, and Avant's fees, which are not on-chain.
