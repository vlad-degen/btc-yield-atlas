## Issues, ranked

### 1. Wrong number (toggle): "All staked ETH" adds back more than the issuers lost, Jul 2025 to Apr 2026
`used_elsewhere_eth` (04_chapter.py) sums every ledger reason starting `staking token`. That includes:
- `staking tokens in lending markets beyond the issuer's backing` rows. These are cuts from money markets, not from the issuer, but are booked `to=<issuer>`.
- the full `staking token ... held/posted` amounts in months where the issuer row was floored at 0 (`held exceeds issuer row`).
So the toggle adds back about twice the floored excess. ether.fi with the toggle on, against DefiLlama gross: 2.91M vs 2.49M (2025-07), 3.14M vs 2.70M (2025-12), 3.23M vs 2.62M (2026-01). Affected issuer-months: ether.fi 2025-05 to 2026-02, kelp 2025-09 and 2025-12 to 2026-04, renzo 2026-01. Excess per month runs from 55k to 224k ETH (ether.fi) and up to 73k ETH (kelp, 2026-04).
**Fix:** in 04_chapter.py, exclude the `staking tokens in lending markets beyond` reason, and subtract the `held exceeds issuer row` amount for the same issuer and month. Equivalently, set used = issuer gross before netting − net.

### 2. Wrong number (toggle, small): some netting is missing from the ledger, so the toggle adds back too little
At T, issuer gross (DefiLlama screen) − net − toggle add-back leaves about 57k ETH unexplained. Lido: 33,185 = EigenLayer's stETH × net/gross (18.5k) + Symbiotic's wstETH × net/gross (14.7k). The rest: rETH 4.2k, ETHx 4.3k, weETH 3.4k, osETH 1.5k, sfrxETH 1.0k. Cause: `03_build.py` step 4 (`sub[iss] += x * net / gross` for restaking platforms) writes no ledger row. Also left out of the toggle: `carry outside lending markets` (Lido 6,014, the Liquity trove) and `product token held` (OETH 1,609, Mellow 1,254).
**Fix:** write a ledger row in step 4 with a reason that starts `staking token`, and include the carry_off reason in `used`. Counting once is not affected. This only changes the toggle and the ledger's completeness.

### 4. Possible double count (not measured): vault rows that hold lending positions
Farming and credit vault rows keep their full DefiLlama value. The lending layer and the issuer subtraction both see the same positions. If such a vault posts wstETH on Aave, Lido loses it twice: once as a lending token, once as "held by a counted product". The vault row counts it once, and so does the lending layer. If the position is a loop (A1), the ETH sits in both the vault row and Leveraged staking, and both are on by default. Rows in scope at T: Enzyme 17.7k, IPOR Fusion 14.6k, Alchemix V3 12.2k, Upshift 11.1k, Veda other 10.0k, Vesper other 7.7k, Lagoon 6.3k, Mellow other 3.4k, Cap 22.7k. Upper bound about 0.1M ETH (under 0.7% of the total). REPORT.md:97 lists this as a limit but says only that money markets is an upper bound. If these vaults loop, the default total is also affected.
**Fix:** read these vaults' accounts in the lending-split capture, as product_accounts.py already does for carry products. Otherwise, reword the limit to say Leveraged staking can be affected too.

Restaking moves under 20% only because other rows offset it. Same spike-and-revert in Morpho Blue (2025-03) and Fluid lending (2026-04), both in money markets (off).
