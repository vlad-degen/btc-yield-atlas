# What backs the five ETH carry books, and what an investor could actually withdraw

The five product books do not give an investor five equivalent cash claims. Concrete accepts the tested withdrawals into a queue. Liquid has a substantial record of Ethereum queue payments, but its cross-chain portfolio was not unwound in an aggregate simulation. Rocksolid rejects new redemption requests in its recorded Closing state. Liquity's configured route passes the 1% withdrawal simulation and fails at 10% and 30%. Royco's immediate withdrawal route fails at all three sizes.

This review keeps the financial boundary at **2 October 2026, 23:59:59 UTC**. Ethereum reads use block **26,108,081**, whose timestamp equals that boundary. Monad reads use block **110,031,481**. Public contract sources and archive responses were captured on **4 October**. Later observations are identified separately. All calls were read-only; no transaction was signed or broadcast. The [machine-readable ledger](../../../data/eth/backing_exit_closure.json) retains exact callers, arguments, responses, values and limitations. The [source manifest](../../../data/eth/backing_exit_manifest.json) hashes every captured file, including unsuccessful attempts.

## Book value and the positions examined

Book NAV is the product's recorded net asset value. The reconstruction below adds the specific positions examined, subtracts debt and eliminates internal receipts. It includes protocol oracle values, stablecoin claims marked at a nominal dollar and nested vault accounting. It is not a liquidation-value estimate. ETH display prices use the study's $2,667.9504418816 quote, observed one second after the financial boundary; the same price convention is retained throughout.

| Product | Whole book, USD | Examined reconstruction, USD | Book less this reconstruction, USD | What this figure measures |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Concrete Delta weETH | $820,029,052.88 | $480,616,981.10 | $339,412,071.78 | Conditional ceiling from visible shared custody; Delta's ownership allocation is unproven |
| ether.fi Liquid ETH, original reconstruction | $472,683,616.62 | $460,230,987.03 | $12,452,629.59 | Original mixed-price positions, including the Monad receipt at its parent book value |
| ether.fi Liquid ETH, expanded nested look-through | $472,683,616.62 | $458,585,635.03 | $14,097,981.60 | Replaces that Monad receipt with the specific local and remote claims examined |
| Rocksolid rETH | $25,953,201.21 | $6,101,466.15 | $19,851,735.06 | Known execution-wallet cash, Spark net equity and identified pool or vault claims |
| Liquity ETH Carry | $16,045,058.59 | $16,029,875.52 | $15,183.06 | Trove equity, cash, Curve principal and a cached Uniswap V4 market mark |
| Royco ETH | $309,619.94 | $309,386.09 | $233.85 | Fresh Morpho loan position, senior receipt and captured cash/accounting marks |

An unmapped amount is not evidence of a loss. It can reflect omitted positions, custody or attribution records, liabilities, and different valuation methods. Conversely, a small difference does not establish cash solvency. Royco and Liquity both retain nominal-dollar and nested or cached accounting assumptions. The rows also overlap economically: Rocksolid owns a Liquity receipt. Adding these reconstructions as independent market capital would count some claims twice.

## Concrete: shared custody and an accepted request without cash

The [shared strategy Safe](https://etherscan.io/address/0x7ee29373f075ee1d83b1b93b4fe94ae242df5178) has $503.453 million of Aave collateral and $105.736 million of debt, giving $397.716 million of protocol-marked net equity. Direct token balances add $46.333 million. The s0xUSD and ctDefiUSDT receipts add conditional book claims of $10.391 million and $26.176 million. Their underlying custody and cash redemption are not independently reconciled here.

The Safe owns all shares of the sibling ctwstETH+ wrapper and also holds that wrapper's custody assets. Counting the sibling receipt in addition to those assets would invent a second asset. The reconstruction eliminates it. Even assigning every examined shared asset to Delta leaves **$339.412 million**, or **41.390%** of Delta's book, unmapped. Since beneficial assignment is not public, Delta's attributable share of this visible inventory has a conditional range of **$0 to $480.617 million**. The specific missing record is an asset and liability schedule allocating the shared custody account to Delta, including any private holdings. [Position evidence](../../../data/eth/concrete_lookthrough_T.json), [new fixed-block balances](../../../raw/eth/research-closure-2026-10-04/exit/backing_token_balances_T.json).

All three tested `withdraw` calls return successfully from the sole share holder. The active queue and captured [Concrete implementation source](https://etherscan.io/address/0xb9dc54c8261745cb97070cefbe3d3d815aee8f20#code) explain the result: shares enter epoch escrow and a withdrawal is queued; that return is not a weETH transfer. The epoch-one claim call reverts because the epoch is not processed. The complete wrapper event census from block 23,996,053 through T contains no queued withdrawal or cash-claim event. This establishes the examined on-chain record, without making a claim about private payment channels. [Calls](../../../raw/eth/research-closure-2026-10-04/exit/request_and_claim_simulations_T.json), [event census](../../../raw/eth/research-closure-2026-10-04/exit/concrete_exitlogs_T.json).

## Liquid: the Monad claim is a receipt, not cash

The original reconstruction includes a **$21.687 million** book claim on [Liquid's Monad vault](https://monadscan.com/address/0xa024063b630d554078bbf985718b22f3c6870ee0). Looking through that receipt identifies 7,427.621860 SteakETH shares, with an approximately **7,495.062795 WETH** accounting claim, 0.259908 wstETH and 26.3816 USDC on Monad. The same address also holds **16.655292 WETH on Ethereum**. These specific claims total **$20.042 million** under the stated price assumptions. Another 30,252.912848 WMON is identified but unpriced.

Replacing the parent receipt with this examined subtotal reduces the reconstruction by **$1.645 million**. It does not add remote assets to the receipt a second time. That produces the expanded **$458.586 million** subtotal and **$14.098 million** scope residual. Completing the nested reconciliation requires the full local and remote asset-and-liability inventory, bridge claims and a WMON valuation. SteakETH's own borrower backing also remains a separate layer. Zero locally issued Monad vault shares do not establish an empty remote vault. [Remote balances](../../../raw/eth/research-closure-2026-10-04/exit/monad_token_claims_T.json), [local balances](../../../raw/eth/research-closure-2026-10-04/exit/remaining_known_claims_T.json).

SteakETH provides a useful warning about relying on getters. Its `maxWithdraw` returns **zero**, yet a one-WETH `withdraw` call from the actual vault address returns successfully. Both responses are retained. A successful return without a simulated transfer trace and a bound T implementation does not prove executable cash liquidity. The getter alone does not resolve the discrepancy either. [Exact calls](../../../raw/eth/research-closure-2026-10-04/exit/monad_steak_exit_T.json).

The original residual also cannot be filled by applying an official SPK price to the large token named SPARK. The held contract `0xe92e...09acb` identifies a different token named 星火通证. No price was obtained for that exact asset. The token-address distinction matters more than the symbol.

Ordinary Liquid share holders cannot call the vault's direct exit as authorized managers. The Ethereum queue additionally requires share approval. Aggregate requests for 1%, 10% and 30% of the consolidated Ethereum/Optimism book were **not tested**; their absence is not a rejected request. Historical cash is much stronger evidence for the examined Ethereum route: **2,822 solved requests** all have exact token-payment matches, across **887 successful receipts**. They paid **126,695.007458 eETH** and **39,220.644015 weETH** in native units. The census has 2,916 requests, 72 cancellations and 22 pending requests. Completed waits have a **12.56-hour median**, **56.39-hour p90** and **294.14-hour maximum**. Those statistics exclude pending and cancelled requests, older queues and Optimism. [Request and payment evidence](../../../raw/eth/research-closure-2026-10-04/exit/liquid_queue_exitlogs_T.json), [verified receipts](../../../raw/eth/research-closure-2026-10-04/exit/liquid_queue_payout_receipts_repaired.json).

## Rocksolid: known net positions and a distinct Closing state

The $6.101 million examined subtotal includes $0.856 million of loose rETH, weETH and USDT, a $1.944 million Liquity book receipt, $0.811 million of Spark net equity, $2.235 million of Balancer principal, $0.252 million of YieldBasis WETH LT claims and $4,372.58 of native ETH. Spark's **$7.108 million collateral less $6.297 million debt** is counted once. Its interest-bearing receipt and variable-debt token are not additional assets or liabilities. The Balancer gauge claim maps to pool shares; pool live balances are already adjusted for token exchange rates, so those rates are not applied twice. YieldBasis is valued through the gauge's LT claim and the LT's WETH book price. [Spark and pool reads](../../../raw/eth/research-closure-2026-10-04/exit/known_nested_claims_T.json), [pool identity and principal](../../../raw/eth/research-closure-2026-10-04/exit/explicit_pool_claims_T.json).

The remaining **$19.852 million**, or **76.491%** of the book, has no attributable position in this bounded inventory. Resolving it requires an identified position or custody schedule, rather than another generic token symbol search. The 14.964423 rETH held by the vault itself is disclosed separately as an exit reserve. Settled redemption reserves can already have been removed from managed `totalAssets`; adding that cash again without tracing its allocation could double-count it.

The historical implementation is bound to the T proxy slot and matching runtime bytecode. An [owner transaction](https://etherscan.io/tx/0xb3ed2719618ec664e52d904c4f65eb9dcbbddd7ff1ae5c5599148bbadf23b70a) called `initiateClosing` at **2026-09-29 02:39:23 UTC**. Its state enum is **Closing**, which rejects new requests. The verified code uses an owner-initiated `initiateClosing` followed by `close`; it is distinct from routine `settleRedeem` and has no reopening function in this implementation. That observation does not establish permanent economic deprecation, failure or final investor outcome. A separately dated 4 October state-event query returned no later transition. [State, transaction and implementation binding](../../../raw/eth/research-closure-2026-10-04/exit/rocksolid_implementation_binding_T.json).

The new request calls fail with `NotOpen(1)`. Separate immediate withdrawal calls fail with arithmetic `Panic(0x11)` because the examined holder has no claimable redemption amount. These are different methods and different failures. Earlier cash history remains valid: **428 payments** totaling **4,326.992056 rETH** all match token transfers. **427** can be uniquely linked to a preceding request with the same controller and share amount. Every linked settlement precedes its cash payment. On that common set, request-to-settlement has a **16.89-hour median**, while request-to-collected-cash has a **40.30-hour median** and **160.27-hour p90**. The longest cash wait, **3,239.36 hours**, can include the investor's delay in claiming. One unpairable cash claim is excluded from wait statistics. [Historical events](../../../raw/eth/research-closure-2026-10-04/exit/rocksolid_exitlogs_T.json), [cash receipts](../../../raw/eth/research-closure-2026-10-04/exit/rocksolid_payout_receipts_repaired.json).

## Liquity: the actual debt currency and pool coins reconcile most of the book

The active Ebisu position has **4,585.482153 wstETH** collateral and **6,752,064.750858 ebUSD** debt. At the standardized study price and nominal-dollar debt mark, that is $8.484 million of equity. Loose WETH/ebUSD add $0.802 million. The stored Uniswap V4 market value adds $0.159 million.

The identified Curve pool's legacy symbol says BOLDUSDC, but its actual contracts identify **ebUSD and USDC**. Its balances are 7.261 million ebUSD and 2.782 million USDC. Liquity's gauge receipt and tiny direct pool receipt represent **$6.584 million** of pro-rata principal if both coins are worth a dollar. Adding that principal brings the conditional reconstruction to $16.030 million and leaves $15,183.06, or **0.0946%**, unreconciled. This is a price-and-book reconciliation, not proof that every claim can be paid at that mark. The historical use of every borrowed unit has not been completely traced. [Actual pool contract](https://etherscan.io/address/0xefc6516323fbd28e80b85a497b65a86243a54b3e#code), [fixed-block coin, gauge and balance reads](../../../raw/eth/research-closure-2026-10-04/exit/explicit_pool_claims_T.json).

The actual large holder's **60.140017 WETH** withdrawal, 1% of the book, succeeds in a non-persistent simulation. The 10% and 30% calls revert on the configured path despite sufficient holder shares. This measures that route at those sizes; it does not measure the capacity of every possible manager unwind. The separate historical census contains **93 verified cash payments totaling 516.833393 WETH**, paid in the withdrawal transaction. The successful simulation is not included as an actual payment. [Withdrawal simulations](../../../raw/eth/research-closure-2026-10-04/exit/withdraw_simulations_T.json), [actual cash receipts](../../../raw/eth/research-closure-2026-10-04/exit/liquity_payout_receipts_repaired.json).

## Royco: fresh venue reads replace stale strategy accounting

Caliber's `isAccountingFresh` is false at T and `getNetAum` reverts with `PositionAccountingStale`. The last actual [position-accounting transaction](https://etherscan.io/tx/0x2b2a612c368d787f558ef85168d3b5622d66b456997c2e2e6e22898f5654e856) binds the loan to a specific [Morpho Blue market](https://app.morpho.org/ethereum/market/0xf5c5df23559b0fb56560a7578ea17d81e245153ba64b8132df026c9358864d27). Fresh T reads show **93.075979 wstETH collateral** and **90,890.891657 PYUSD debt**, after pending Morpho interest is accrued to the block timestamp. The contemporaneous borrow-rate quote annualizes to **30.2424% APR**; it is not a forecast or an observed year-long cost.

The loan equity is combined with **$90,476.22** of srRoyUSDC senior-vault accounting value, **$354.67** of loose wstETH and **$185.29** of captured PYUSD base-token accounting value. The resulting subtotal is $309,386.09, leaving $233.85, or **0.0755%**, against the parent book. The physical PYUSD dust quantity was not separately decoded; excluding that accounting value gives **$309,200.81** and a **$419.13** difference. Both versions retain the senior-credit receipt and nominal dollar assumptions. Neither establishes senior-credit backing or executable cash. The accounting instructions connect the loan venue and destination receipt, without tracing each PYUSD unit through swaps into USDC. [Fresh loan and claim reads](../../../raw/eth/research-closure-2026-10-04/exit/remaining_known_claims_T.json), [interest calculation inputs](../../../raw/eth/research-closure-2026-10-04/exit/royco_morpho_accrual_T.json).

The machine and Caliber beacon implementations at T both exactly match captured verified runtime bytecode. The duration setter's mapped AccessManager role is active through a **3-of-4 Safe**, with a **three-day execution delay**. An unscheduled execution of the duration change reverts. The currently configured instruction-root delay is **two days**, governing a different action. These observations do not establish an immutable delay or a universally additive five-day notice. They also do not audit every administrator or module route. [Mapped roles, caller and scheduling simulation](../../../raw/eth/research-closure-2026-10-04/exit/royco_authority_active_T.json).

The examined wrapper's immediate withdrawal calls all fail, with zero maximum immediate withdrawal. The complete wrapper log interval has no request or cash-payment event. The srRoyUSDC nested receipt's own cash redemption was not simulated, so its book value cannot be used as evidence that a Royco holder could collect cash at T.

## Offline reproduction and coverage

From the repository root, run:

```sh
python3 tools/eth/backing_exit_build.py
python3 tools/eth/backing_exit_verify.py
```

The builder calls no network. It uses the immutable scoped financial inputs, preserved primary captures and pure interest math. The verifier checks file hashes, unchanged book NAVs, net reconstruction sums, demand denominators, complete event-query intervals, exact historical payout matches, and the request-before-settlement-before-cash invariant. All **838 raw files**, including retries and errors, remain archived. Only successful records are used as evidence. Failed requests do not establish missing cash. The manifest separates inputs, builders and outputs so the result can be reproduced without trusting a later live explorer page.
