# ETH research depth expansion

The end product is an English market research website following the eight BTC chapters. The work is judged by resolved financial questions and reproducible evidence, rather than article or audit counts.

1. Identify the meaningful income mechanisms and material products. Trace staking, restaking, lending, ETH debt loops, dollar carry, maturity products, LP, options, basis, incentive and custody layers. Retain historical, closed and recovery cases with their actual status.
2. Broaden actual financing evidence. Read dollar reserves, ETH collateral rules, rates, cash and caps at fixed T across major chains. Enumerate Compound, Euler, Fluid and Silo routes. Keep whole-vault debt separate from ETH-only pair debt.
3. Close selected carry lifecycles. Verify historical nested loan layers, borrow-to-deposit receipts, shares, redemptions, residual liabilities and onward transfers. Report realized leg results only where cash and debt reconcile. Investigate attribution limits instead of inventing owner identities.
4. Measure additional material product histories. Read funded capital, share supply, conversion, fee state and withdrawal evidence for the major missed product families at T and dated earlier blocks. Separate share-value return, externally paid rewards and actual exit proceeds.
5. Integrate and validate the presentation. Preserve Answer, Market, Carry math, Top 5, Other carry, Risks, Playbook and Data. Use bar charts, human English, visible date/unit boundaries and inspectable transactions. Rebuild offline, verify original BTC and captures, exercise desktop/mobile interactions, and package the current site with matching QA.

Completion boundaries stay explicit. A mechanism atlas is not a complete product census; public credit debt is not automatically carry capital; private or confidential ownership cannot be assigned without evidence. Public coverage omissions remain open research items rather than being reclassified as inherently unavailable.
