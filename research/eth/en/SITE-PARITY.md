# The ETH site and Bitcoin Research

The presentation follows the eight chapters and exhibit order specified in the Bitcoin Research reference. The original Bitcoin HTML, builders, datasets and interactions were inspected alongside the supplied chapter specification.

The detailed strategy atlas, physical-backing ledger, additional financial histories, hgETH loan book, funding by chain, nested carry and traced repayments remain in `exhibits.html` and linked articles. They do not add extra chapters to the main reading route.

## Shared interactions

Counting is available in the header and market chapter. Reset restores the default selection; Escape closes the header menu. The selection is stored in the URL and browser. Category toggles update the numerator, denominator, composition, history and findings together. Excluded categories remain searchable.

Monthly charts expose their table and downloadable selected data. Product tabs support keyboard navigation and deep links. The website includes dark mode, narrow-screen layouts and direct access to source records and detailed articles.

## ETH-specific accounting

ETH already pays staking income, so the carry calculator includes an explicitly dated staking baseline. Borrowing ETH to buy more staking exposure is different from borrowing dollars against ETH. Spot/short basis hedges the underlying ETH price and has a dollar return denominator.

The market measure is overlapping ETH-family exposure. Lending and CDP are off by default as optional financing layers. Basis remains unmeasured in the frozen adapter ledger; its later Ethena disclosure is separate. Product book NAVs are not added on top of their protocol parents. Historical portfolio weights, private payouts and unique external capital are not inferred from totalAssets.

A missing observation stays missing. Contract deployment, a funded balance, a zero adapter token subtotal and a product shutdown are different events. No closure is asserted without evidence.
