# The five largest measured carry-design products

The main Top 5 ranks **Concrete Delta weETH, ether.fi Liquid ETH, Rocksolid rETH, Liquity ETH Carry and Royco ETH** by published whole-product book NAV at the frozen snapshot. All five have primary published carry-design evidence. This is a ranking within the examined census, not a complete census of every ETH product or a ranking by independently verified external equity.

## Why these five

Concrete Delta and Liquid dominate measured parent capital. Rocksolid combines published lending, dollar carry and liquidity allocations. Liquity and Royco are smaller dedicated designs with identifiable contracts and archived share/accounting values. A current product label still requires live collateral, debt and reinvestment verification at T.

Every product is presented in the same order: metrics, money flow, actors and control, income payers, capital/return history, share-holder distribution, borrowing legs and a timeline. Primary contract links sit beside the diagrams and tables. The evidence dataset retains scope and dates.

[Top 5 chapter evidence](../../../data/eth/product_chapters.json).

## What the ranking does not establish

Whole-product book NAV is not carry sleeve capital, dollar loan principal or independent outside deposits. The Concrete products share custody; initial mint and migration events require ownership reconciliation. Liquid includes ETH loops and liquidity alongside dollar carry. Rocksolid's dated allocation report cannot determine its allocation at another block.

Shares are claims rather than people. The holder distributions use reconciled transfer ledgers at T; a wallet can be a custodian, contract or exchange. Ethereum and Optimism circulation belong to one logical Liquid product and need cross-chain reconciliation.

## Supporting comparisons

Fluid Lite, Treehouse and CIAN remain in the comparison of ETH returns and in the detailed library. Their examined mechanism is an ETH-debt loop rather than automatically dollar carry. They help test whether additional strategy complexity beats unlevered staking after denomination and exit costs.

Reservoir and TAU stay in the full carry census and dedicated capital history. Concrete wstETH Plus and the Midas candidates remain separate where carry allocation or product route is unverified. Ethena's hedged ETH backing belongs to basis, with a separately dated observation. No minimum-size threshold removes a measured dedicated product from the table.
